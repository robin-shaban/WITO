# Fonts

This site self-hosts Inter and JetBrains Mono. It makes no request to Google Fonts or to
any other third party.

| Family | Version | Copyright |
|---|---|---|
| Inter | 4.000 | Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors |
| JetBrains Mono | 2.304 | Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors |

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full licence text for
each accompanies the font files in this directory, as `Inter-OFL.txt` and
`JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt`, and must travel with them wherever they are served.

The `.woff2` files here are subsets of the upstream families, covering Basic Latin,
Latin-1 Supplement, Latin Extended-A, general punctuation, currency and arrows. Subsetting
is a permitted modification under the licence. Neither family declares a Reserved Font
Name, so the derived files keep their family names.
